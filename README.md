Robert Half International is the world's first and largest specialized staffing and talent solutions firm. Founded in 1948, it provides contract, temporary, and permanent placement solutions across finance and accounting, technology, marketing, legal, and administrative fields. The company is the parent of Protiviti, a global consulting firm. Listed in the Fortune 500.

**Website:** [https://www.roberthalf.com](https://www.roberthalf.com)

**LinkedIn:** [https://www.linkedin.com/company/robert-half-international](https://www.linkedin.com/company/robert-half-international)

## API Status

Robert Half International does not publicly expose a developer API. Integration with Robert Half's systems is available through partner programs and ATS ecosystem connectors. This repository profiles the company's public presence, products, and services for research and reference purposes.

## Practice Areas

- **Finance & Accounting** — CFOs, controllers, accountants, financial analysts
- **Technology** — Software developers, cybersecurity, data scientists, IT managers
- **Marketing & Creative** — Brand managers, digital marketers, designers
- **Legal** — Attorneys, paralegals, compliance officers
- **Administrative** — Executive assistants, office managers, data entry

## Subsidiaries

- **Protiviti** — [https://www.protiviti.com](https://www.protiviti.com) — Global consulting firm providing internal audit, risk management, and business consulting
