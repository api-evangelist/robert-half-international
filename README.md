# Robert Half International

Robert Half International is the world's first and largest specialized staffing and talent solutions firm. Founded in 1948, it provides contract, temporary, and permanent placement solutions across finance and accounting, technology, marketing, legal, and administrative fields. The company is the parent of Protiviti, a global consulting firm. Listed in the Fortune 500.
